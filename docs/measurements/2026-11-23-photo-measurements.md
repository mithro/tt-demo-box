# Measurements from Tim's photos (2026-11-23)

Twelve photos of the Shenzhen monitor and an ETR dev board, with an Evil Mad Scientist inch
ruler and a Microchip cm/inch PCB ruler in frame. The photos are in Tim's album (link not
recorded here); local copies were taken at 2400 px wide. Figures below are read off the
photos and are good to about +/-2 mm; they are `confirmed=False` until caliper-checked.

## Tim's "Taobao" monitor: a 7" car-style "TFT color monitor", not a 5"

| What | Value | How |
|---|---|---|
| Front face W x H | ~176-181 x ~113 mm | photo 5: inch ruler lying on the face, 192.5 px/in; width cross-checked against the Microchip inch scale (187 px/in) |
| Back W x H | ~182 x ~117-118 mm | photo 11: face-down, inch ruler on the table, 224.5 px/in |
| Screen window | ~159 x ~86 mm | photo 5; matches the common 7" 16:9 panel (154 x 86 active) |
| Depth | NOT measured | no side-on photo |
| Buttons | front face, along one long edge: power, VOL, MENU, -, +, plus an IR window | photos 1, 2, 5 |
| Cable | hard-wired, leaves the **long edge on the button side, near the IR-window corner** | photos 1, 2, 11 |
| Back | flat, two speaker grilles, a stand-clip bracket (slot) centred on one long edge, four screws | photos 10-12 |
| Printed name | "TFT color monitor" | front bezel |

This is the survey's item H (7" plastic car-style monitor, AliExpress 1005004614276566,
173 x 113 x 20 at the rim), or a sibling of it.

## The dev board

"Tiny Tapeout Demoboard: ETR", purple, OSHWA UID CA000047, with a TinyVGA **v1.2** on the
OUTPUT Pmod and a straight VGA plug (photos 6-9). Board ~85 x ~80 mm on photo 8's ruler
(285 px/in), so most likely v3.2. The TinyVGA reaches ~53 mm from the board edge to the face of
its DE-15; the straight plug and its shell add ~40 mm more, before the cable bends (photo 8).
